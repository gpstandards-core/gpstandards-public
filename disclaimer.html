Forensic recovery outcomes are inherently variable. We do not guarantee that any specific amount — or any amount — will be identified or recovered in a given engagement. Any statements regarding potential recovery, return on investment, payback periods, or savings are estimates and benchmarks only, and are not promises or assurances of future results.
