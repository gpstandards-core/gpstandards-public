Accessibility Statement

Last reviewed: June 2026

Genesis & Prime Standards LLC ("G&P Standards") is committed to digital accessibility. We believe the people who evaluate our work — chief financial officers, controllers, and their teams — should be able to read and use this site regardless of ability, and we treat that as a standard, not a courtesy.

Our conformance target

We work to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA, the recognized international standard for web accessibility. We test our site against this standard and remediate issues at the source code level rather than relying on overlay widgets, which independent testing and regulators have found do not reliably deliver accessibility.

What we have done

  • Structured the site with proper semantic landmarks and a "skip to main content" link for keyboard and screen-reader users.
  • Associated every form field with a programmatic label, so assistive technology announces each field correctly.
  • Provided visible keyboard focus indicators on all interactive elements.
  • Reviewed color contrast against the WCAG 2.2 AA thresholds and adjusted text and interface colors that did not meet them.
  • Built the site to work with the assistive technology you already use — screen readers such as VoiceOver and NVDA, browser zoom, and keyboard navigation — rather than adding a separate accessibility tool.

Ongoing effort

Accessibility is a continuing process, not a one-time fix. We re-test the site on a regular schedule and review any new page or feature against WCAG 2.2 AA before it is published. No website can claim to be perfectly accessible to every user in every circumstance, and we do not make that claim; we commit instead to identifying and correcting barriers as we find them or as they are reported to us.

Reporting a barrier

If you encounter any part of this site that is difficult to access, or if you need information from it in a different format, please tell us. We take these reports seriously and will work with you to provide the information you need.

Email: office@gpstandards.com
Please include the page address and a short description of the difficulty, and we will respond promptly.
