§ 02 The Problem

The work was done.
The invoice forgot.

Most law firms operate on hourly billing, yet most associates do not capture their day in real time. Calls roll into meetings. Emails are drafted between hearings. A two-hour negotiation gets logged as one. Over a quarter, the gap between work performed and work invoiced compounds into six and seven figures of leakage.

The problem is rarely a lack of discipline. It is a structural mismatch between how legal work happens and how timekeeping systems are designed. The data exists — in calendars, in email metadata, in document version histories, in matter notes. What is missing is a forensic reconstruction of that activity into billable hours your clients have already agreed to pay for.

That reconstruction is what we deliver.

An Illustration

For a thirty-attorney firm billing at $400 per hour on average, recovering one additional hour per attorney per week represents $624,000 in annual revenue.

The math compounds in either direction. The same firm losing one hour per attorney per week is leaving that same figure on the table — every year, indefinitely, with no line item on the financial statements to show for it.

§ 03 The Audit

A written reclamation roadmap,
delivered in seven business days.

The Performance Dossier

A confidential written report, typically thirty to fifty pages, that documents what we found and what we recommend. Specific. Quantified. Actionable.

  • 01 Time-capture gap analysis across the audited period, segmented by attorney and practice area.
  • 02 Reconstructed activity log from calendar, email metadata, and document timestamps you provide.
  • 03 Estimated recoverable revenue, by source, with the methodology shown.
  • 04 Specific process recommendations for closing the gap going forward.
  • 05 A one-hour review call with the founder to walk through findings with your managing partner or COO.

What We Do Not Do

Honesty about scope is part of the engagement. The audit is a diagnostic, not a software product.

  • — We do not access your case management system. You provide read-only data exports.
  • — We do not bill your clients. We tell you which hours could have been billed and let you decide.
  • — We do not provide legal advice. Recommendations are operational.
  • — We do not install software inside your firm. Everything runs on our infrastructure on the data you export.
  • — We do not retain your data after delivery. All artifacts are destroyed within thirty days unless extended in writing.

§ 07 Honest Answers

Questions partners ask
before they sign.

Do you have prior clients we can speak with? +

Not yet. G&P Standards is a new firm and the early engagements have not yet completed. Our first three clients receive a reduced fee in exchange for permission to publish an anonymized case study after delivery. If you require active client references before signing, we are not yet the right vendor for you, and we will say so on the discovery call.

Are you SOC 2 or ISO certified? +

Not at this time. We are an early-stage consultancy and we will not claim certifications we do not hold. Our security posture for this engagement is built around minimizing what we receive: you control the export, you redact what you choose to redact, and we destroy all artifacts within thirty days of delivery. For firms with vendor policies that strictly require SOC 2 Type II at signing, we are not yet the right vendor.

How do you protect our data? +

We sign a mutual NDA before any data exchange. We never receive direct access to your case management system — you provide read-only exports of the data we agree on, in a format you control. Data is processed on encrypted infrastructure and is not used to train any AI model. Within thirty days of delivery, all data and intermediate artifacts are destroyed, with a written certification of destruction.

What AI do you use? Does it train on our data? +

We use commercial AI tools, principally Anthropic's Claude on the API tier, which has a zero data retention policy when configured accordingly. We do not use consumer chat products for client data. Your data is not used to train any model, by us or by any provider in our stack. The data processing terms are included in our standard engagement letter.

What if you do not find significant leakage? +

If the audit identifies less than $25,000 in estimated recoverable revenue for the Standard Audit, or less than $50,000 for the Extended Audit, we refund fifty percent of the fee. The exact thresholds and refund mechanics are documented in the engagement letter you sign before work begins. No verbal promises.

Wesam Mohammed is the founder and operator. Wesam is based in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, with a background in business operations, sales, and analytical work. Why not just buy a billing software product? +

You should, eventually. Software products are excellent at preventing future leakage. They are less effective at reconstructing past leakage, because the past data lives across many systems that no single product is designed to read together. The forensic audit is a one-time diagnostic that informs which products are worth buying, and what process changes make those products effective. The two approaches complement each other.
