## What the Firm Does

G&P Standards operates two practices:

1. **Vendor Contract Leakage Audit** — A fixed-fee forensic engagement reconciling vendor invoices against contract terms over a 24-to-36-month lookback, identifying duplicate payments, missed credits, expired tier discounts, renewal-cap breaches, MFN violations, scope-creep billing, and tax/surcharge misapplication. Standard delivery 4 to 8 weeks. Methodology informed by the AICPA Statement on Standards for Forensic Services (SSFS No. 1); G&P is not a CPA firm and provides no attest services. Every finding is evidenced to source.

2. **Continuous Market Intelligence** — A monthly retainer for ongoing vendor benchmark monitoring, contract-renewal alerts, and competitive intelligence reporting. Offered as a loyalty program to clients who have completed a Vendor Contract Leakage Audit.

The firm also operates as a **named subcontractor, co-branded specialist, or white-labeled execution layer** for Big 4 Transaction Services teams, mid-market M&A advisory boutiques, PE Operating Partners, Independent Sponsors, and Commercial Lenders evaluating borrower vendor risk.
