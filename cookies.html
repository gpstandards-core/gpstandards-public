Cookie Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1 · What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They let a site remember your actions and preferences, measure how the site is used, and — in some cases — support advertising. This policy explains how Genesis & Prime Standards LLC (“G&P Standards”) uses cookies and similar technologies on gpstandards.com, and the choices you have.

2 · The categories we use

Strictly Necessary

Required for the site to function — page navigation, security, and remembering your cookie choices. These cannot be switched off and do not require consent.

Analytics & Performance

Help us understand which pages are viewed and how the site performs, so we can improve it. These run only with your consent and are used in aggregate.

Marketing & Retargeting

Allow us — and advertising platforms we work with — to measure campaigns and show you relevant messages on other sites after you leave ours. These run only with your consent.

3 · Third parties

With your consent, we may use third-party services that set their own cookies, which may include analytics providers (such as Google Analytics) and advertising platforms (such as Meta and Google) used for measurement and retargeting. These providers process data under their own privacy terms. We list active providers here and update this section as our stack changes.

4 · How we use your information — and what we will never do

We do not sell your personal information for money. Your information is used for our own marketing, to contact you about your inquiry, and to improve our services.

Where we share information with advertising platforms to measure campaigns or retarget visitors, certain US state privacy laws (including the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the CPRA) may classify that activity as a “sale” or “sharing” of personal information. You have the right to opt out of that activity at any time — see your choices below.

5 · Your choices & rights

  • —Consent banner. On your first visit you may accept all cookies or limit them to those strictly necessary. You can change your choice at any time using the “Cookie settings” link in the site footer.
  • —Do Not Sell or Share. US residents may opt out of any cookie-based sharing classified as a sale/share by selecting “Essential only” in the banner, or by contacting us at the address below.
  • —Browser controls. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their settings. Blocking strictly-necessary cookies may affect how the site works.
  • —Access & deletion. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable law, by contacting us.

6 · Changes & contact

We may update this Cookie Policy as our practices or applicable law evolve. Material changes will be reflected by the “last updated” date above.

Questions or requests: office@gpstandards.com. This policy works together with our Privacy Policy.
